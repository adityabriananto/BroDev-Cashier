# Business Objectives

**Mission:** PROJECT-001  
**Product:** BroDev Cashier

## Primary Objectives

- Improve retail checkout speed by providing a focused cashier workflow.
- Reduce manual calculation errors for subtotal, tax, payment, and change.
- Improve stock accuracy through product, inventory, and stock movement tracking.
- Support common payment workflows used by local stores.
- Provide business visibility through operational dashboards and reports.

## Operational Objectives

- Enable cashiers to process sales from desktop, laptop, tablet, or mobile-sized screens.
- Enable admins to manage products, pricing, stock, and user access.
- Enable owners or managers to review sales performance and stock conditions.
- Support thermal receipt printing for completed transactions.
- Provide low stock visibility so restocking decisions can happen earlier.

## Engineering Objectives

- Keep the application aligned with Laravel 13, PHP 8.3, Blade, Tailwind CSS, Vite, and PHPUnit.
- Use migrations as the database source of truth.
- Keep dependencies lightweight and justified.
- Add service/domain layers only when business complexity requires them.
- Maintain FlowForge mission deliverables as the engineering planning record.

## Business Success Measures

- Sales can be recorded consistently during daily cashier operations.
- Stock levels can be reviewed without manual spreadsheet reconciliation.
- Payment method usage can be identified from transaction records.
- Reports can support daily and periodic business review.
- New retail workflows can be added without destabilizing existing cashier behavior.
