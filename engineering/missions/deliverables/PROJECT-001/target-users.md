# Target Users

**Mission:** PROJECT-001  
**Product:** BroDev Cashier

## Primary Users

### Cashier

Cashiers use the system to process sales, select products, accept payment, calculate totals, and print receipts. Their interface must be fast, clear, and tolerant of repeated daily use.

### Store Admin

Store admins manage products, prices, stock values, user access, and operational settings. They need reliable forms, validation, and clear feedback when data changes.

### Store Owner or Manager

Owners and managers review business activity, sales performance, stock status, and operational trends. They need dashboards and reports that support daily decisions.

## Secondary Users

### Inventory Staff

Inventory staff may update stock, monitor low stock items, and record stock adjustments as the inventory domain matures.

### Supplier Coordinator

Supplier coordinators may manage supplier information and purchasing workflows in later phases.

## User Needs

- Cashiers need speed and accuracy.
- Admins need control and traceability.
- Owners need visibility and confidence.
- Inventory staff need stock correctness.
- Future business roles need workflows that do not disrupt cashier operations.

## Accessibility and Device Expectations

The product should support desktop and responsive layouts suitable for tablets or mobile-sized screens. Interfaces should avoid unnecessary visual complexity and prioritize readable operational data.
