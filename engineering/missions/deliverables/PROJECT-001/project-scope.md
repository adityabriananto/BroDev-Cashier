# Project Scope

**Mission:** PROJECT-001  
**Product:** BroDev Cashier

## In Scope

BroDev Cashier covers the core workflows needed to operate a small retail point of sale system.

## Functional Scope

- Authentication and role-aware access.
- Cashier sales processing.
- Product catalog management.
- Inventory and stock monitoring.
- Low stock alerting.
- Payment recording for cash, QRIS, and bank transfer.
- Receipt generation and browser-based thermal printing.
- Sales and stock reporting.
- Basic administration for users and settings.

## Data Scope

- Products, categories, and units.
- Customers and suppliers as the domain grows.
- Stock, stock movements, and stock adjustments.
- Sales, sale items, and payments.
- Reporting data derived from operational records.

## Engineering Scope

- Laravel MVC as the initial backend architecture.
- Blade and Tailwind CSS as the MVP frontend approach.
- Vite for frontend bundling.
- Eloquent ORM, migrations, seeders, and factories for the database layer.
- PHPUnit for automated tests.
- FlowForge for mission lifecycle and engineering documentation.

## MVP Priority

The first product phase should prioritize cashier reliability, product and stock correctness, payment recording, and simple operational reporting before expanding to advanced business modules.
