# Glossary

**Mission:** PROJECT-001  
**Product:** BroDev Cashier

## Terms

**Admin**  
A user responsible for managing product, stock, users, and operational settings.

**Bank Transfer**  
A payment method where the customer pays through a bank transfer channel.

**Cashier**  
A user responsible for processing sales transactions.

**Category**  
A product grouping used to organize the catalog.

**Grand Total**  
The final sale amount after subtotal and tax calculations.

**Inventory**  
The available stock and stock activity for sellable products.

**Low Stock Alert**  
A signal that product stock is below a defined threshold.

**Payment**  
The record of how a sale was paid.

**POS**  
Point of Sale, the system used to complete retail checkout operations.

**Product**  
A sellable item with SKU, name, price, and stock information.

**QRIS**  
A QR-based Indonesian payment standard used as one of the supported payment methods.

**Receipt**  
A printed or printable summary of a completed sale.

**Sale**  
A completed checkout transaction.

**Sale Item**  
An individual product line within a sale.

**SKU**  
Stock Keeping Unit, a unique product identifier used by the store.

**Stock Adjustment**  
A manual correction to product stock, usually used for audit, damage, or reconciliation.

**Stock Movement**  
A record of stock increase or decrease caused by sales, purchases, or adjustments.

**Subtotal**  
The sale amount before tax and final total calculation.

**Supplier**  
A business or person that provides products to the store.

**Tax**  
The configured percentage or amount applied to a sale before the grand total.

**Unit**  
The measurement or packaging type for a product.
