# Initial Product Roadmap

**Mission:** PROJECT-001  
**Product:** BroDev Cashier

## Phase 1: Project Definition

Define product vision, business objectives, scope, target users, business domain, requirements, coding standards, development principles, and roadmap.

## Phase 2: Core POS Foundation

- Establish authentication and role-aware access.
- Build product catalog foundations.
- Build cashier sales workflow.
- Persist sales, sale items, and payment records.
- Apply subtotal, tax, grand total, and change calculation rules.
- Prevent sales that exceed available stock.

## Phase 3: Inventory Foundation

- Improve stock tracking.
- Add stock movement records.
- Add stock adjustment workflow.
- Add low stock visibility.
- Prepare inventory data for reporting.

## Phase 4: Receipts and Payment Workflow

- Refine cash, QRIS, and bank transfer payment recording.
- Generate transaction receipts.
- Optimize receipt layout for 58mm thermal printing.
- Validate receipt output from browser printing.

## Phase 5: Reporting and Dashboard

- Add daily sales summary.
- Add sales report.
- Add stock report.
- Add payment method reporting.
- Add profit-oriented reporting when product cost data exists.

## Phase 6: Administration and Settings

- Improve user management.
- Add business settings where needed.
- Add product categories and units if not already complete.
- Prepare customer and supplier foundations.

## Phase 7: Future Expansion

- Purchase management.
- Supplier workflows.
- Customer management.
- Advanced reports.
- Multi-branch evaluation.
- Offline or mobile strategy evaluation after MVP stability.
