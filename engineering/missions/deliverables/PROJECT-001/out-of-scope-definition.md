# Out of Scope Definition

**Mission:** PROJECT-001  
**Product:** BroDev Cashier

## Out of Scope for Initial Product Definition

The following capabilities are intentionally outside the initial scope so the product can focus on stable POS operations.

## Excluded Business Capabilities

- Full accounting ledger.
- Payroll management.
- Human resources management.
- Enterprise resource planning.
- Multi-warehouse optimization.
- Franchise or multi-company consolidation.
- Advanced customer loyalty and campaign automation.
- Marketplace, e-commerce, or online storefront management.
- Hardware driver development for printers, cash drawers, or barcode scanners.

## Excluded Technical Capabilities

- Native mobile applications.
- Offline-first synchronization.
- Real-time distributed inventory across multiple branches.
- Complex data warehouse or business intelligence platform.
- Microservice architecture.
- Custom framework abstraction over Laravel.
- Replacing Blade with a JavaScript SPA during MVP.

## Future Consideration

Some excluded capabilities may become valid later, especially customer management, supplier workflows, purchase management, advanced reporting, and multi-branch support. They should be evaluated through future FlowForge missions after the core POS domain is stable.
