# Product Vision Document

**Mission:** PROJECT-001  
**Product:** BroDev Cashier

## Vision

BroDev Cashier is a web-based point of sale system for small and growing retail businesses that need fast sales processing, reliable inventory visibility, and practical operational reporting in one maintainable Laravel application.

The product should help store operators complete daily cashier work with fewer manual calculations, fewer stock mistakes, and clearer visibility into business activity.

## Product Positioning

BroDev Cashier is positioned as an operational POS application, not a general accounting suite or enterprise resource planning platform. The product focuses on the retail transaction workflow first, then expands carefully into inventory, purchasing, reporting, and administration.

## Core Value

- Process sales quickly and consistently.
- Track product stock and stock movement.
- Support common local payment methods such as cash, QRIS, and bank transfer.
- Provide clear operational data for cashiers, admins, and owners.
- Keep the engineering foundation simple, testable, and maintainable.

## Product Principles

- Cashier workflows must remain fast and low-friction.
- Inventory data must be trustworthy because it affects sales and restocking decisions.
- Reporting must be useful for daily business decisions, not only technical completeness.
- The system should follow Laravel conventions unless there is a clear business reason to do otherwise.
- MVP scope should favor stable retail operations over broad but shallow features.

## Success Indicators

- Cashiers can complete sales without manual subtotal, tax, or grand total calculations.
- Admins can manage product and stock data without direct database access.
- Owners can review sales and stock information from application reports.
- The codebase remains aligned with the PROJECT-000 Laravel, Blade, Tailwind CSS, Vite, PHPUnit, and FlowForge baseline.
