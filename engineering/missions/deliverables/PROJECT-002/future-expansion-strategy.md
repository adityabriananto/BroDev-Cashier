# Future Expansion Strategy

**Mission:** PROJECT-002  
**Product:** BroDev Cashier

## Purpose

This document defines how the BroDev Cashier business domain can expand after the core POS domain is stable.

## Expansion Principle

Future expansion should protect the cashier workflow. New domains should improve business capability without making checkout slower, less reliable, or harder to operate.

## Near-Term Expansion

### Customer Domain

Introduce customer concepts for sales history, customer identity, and future loyalty workflows.

### Supplier Domain

Introduce supplier concepts for restocking, purchasing, and vendor tracking.

### Purchase Domain

Introduce purchasing concepts to explain stock increases from supplier activity.

### Settings Domain

Clarify configurable business concepts such as tax behavior, receipt identity, and operational preferences.

## Mid-Term Expansion

### Stock Reconciliation

Add richer stock audit concepts, including discrepancy review and adjustment approval.

### Returns and Voids

Add controlled business concepts for reversing or canceling completed sales.

### Promotions

Introduce discount and promotion concepts only after pricing and cashier flows are stable.

### Profit Reporting

Introduce cost-aware reporting after product cost and purchase concepts exist.

## Long-Term Expansion

### Multi-Branch Operations

Introduce branch concepts only after single-store operations are stable and well understood.

### Advanced Analytics

Expand reporting into trends, forecasts, and inventory planning after reliable operational data exists.

### Offline Strategy

Evaluate offline-first cashier behavior only when business needs justify the added complexity.

## Domain Governance

- Each new domain should have clear ownership and boundaries.
- New concepts should be added through future FlowForge missions.
- Expansion should not mix accounting, inventory, sales, and reporting concerns without clear boundaries.
- Business terms should be added to the glossary as the domain matures.
